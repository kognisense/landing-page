## FAQ Section

**Headline:**
Frequently Asked Questions

**Subheadline:**
Everything you need to know about Docuparse ESG and UK mandatory ESG reporting requirements for 2026.

### Questions & Answers:

**Q: Who is required to comply with UK SDR 2026?**
A: Companies with over £36 million in turnover or 250+ employees must comply with SECR (Streamlined Energy and Carbon Reporting). Suppliers to UK retailers (Tesco, M&S, Sainsbury's) face additional Scope 3 reporting obligations starting January 2026.

**Q: How does Docuparse ESG handle my sensitive supplier data?**
A: Your data stays 100% private. We use VPC isolation in AWS London regions, ensuring your proprietary supplier information never leaves the UK and is never used to train public AI models.

Each customer's data is completely siloed in isolated virtual private cloud environments with enterprise-grade encryption.

**Q: What documents can Docuparse ESG process?**
A: We extract data from utility bills (electricity, gas, water), fuel invoices, delivery receipts, fleet mileage logs, waste management reports, and commodity purchase orders. Supported formats include PDF, Excel, CSV, and scanned images.

**Q: How accurate is the AI extraction compared to manual data entry?**
A: Our AWS Bedrock models are designed for high accuracy on structured documents like invoices and utility bills. All extractions include confidence scores, and flagged items can be manually reviewed before final report generation to ensure data quality.

**Q: Can I export data in formats required by auditors and regulators?**
A: Yes. Docuparse ESG generates audit-ready reports in Excel (SECR templates), PDF (s172 strategic reports), and CSV for GHG Protocol submissions. All outputs include source document references and calculation methodologies required by the FRC.

**Q: What is the pricing and when will Docuparse ESG be available?**
